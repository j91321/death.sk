I'm a security researcher based in Slovakia, primarily focusing on **detection engineering and threat hunting** (hence the blog's name DEATH). For the past several years I've spent an unhealthy amount of time thinking about EDRs, as I lead the EDR R&D team at ESET. I've also been heavily involved in architecting ESET's MDR service, and I work on incident response and digital forensics. Before that, I was a SOC monkey and a malware analyst.

Some notable experiences:

- Locked Shields and Crossed Swords cybersecurity exercises, organized by the NATO CCDCOE
- Cyber Europe exercise, organized by ENISA
- Several rounds of MITRE ATT&CK Evaluations (which I hate with a passion)
- Various AV-Comparatives tests
